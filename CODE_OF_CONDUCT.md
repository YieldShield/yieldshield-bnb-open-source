# Code of Conduct

YieldShield aims to keep project discussions respectful, focused and safe for contributors.

Be constructive and specific. Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and communication styles. Keep technical disagreement about the work rather than the person, and handle security-sensitive information responsibly.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted attention, publishing someone else's private information and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to david@yieldshield.ai. Report vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not post exploit details in public.
